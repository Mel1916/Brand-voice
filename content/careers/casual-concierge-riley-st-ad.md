# Position Description — Casual Concierge (x2)

**Location:** 22 Riley St
**Employment type:** Casual
**Term:** Now through end of December
**Availability required:** Thursday to Sunday

---

## About the Role

We're opening our doors at 22 Riley St, and we want the first face our customers see to be a great one. As a Concierge, you'll set the tone the moment someone walks in — warm, welcoming, and ready to help them feel right at home before they've even reached the counter.

This is a casual role running now through the end of December, perfect for someone who loves people, thrives in a fast-paced retail environment, and wants to be part of a brand-new store from day one.

## What You'll Do

- Greet every customer with warmth and make them feel genuinely welcome from the moment they arrive
- Create a five-star first impression of the store and the York Jewellers experience
- Help customers find their way — directing them to the right person, product, or service
- Support the retail team on the floor, especially during busy periods
- Keep the store presentation polished and inviting throughout the day
- Stay energetic and engaged on your feet for extended periods, including during peak Christmas trade

## What We're Looking For

- A friendly, approachable and confident personality
- Energy and enthusiasm you bring to every shift
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Comfort engaging with customers and creating a welcoming experience
- Reliability and punctuality — we're counting on you
- Ability to stand and stay active for extended periods
- Availability Thursday through Sunday, now through end of December
- Previous experience in retail, hospitality, customer service or concierge roles is preferred, but a great attitude matters most

## Why Join Us

Be part of the team bringing York Jewellers to Riley St from the very beginning. It's a chance to be at the centre of something new, work alongside a supportive team, and help create memorable moments for every customer who walks through our doors this festive season.
