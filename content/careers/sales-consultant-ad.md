At York Jewellers, every sale is a moment — an engagement, an anniversary, a gift that means something. As our next Sales Consultant, you'll be the person who makes that moment feel special, guiding customers to the piece that's right for them and helping them feel confident in their choice.

This is a permanent part-time role for someone who's confident, energetic, and genuinely loves working with people — and who's ready to grow with us.

- Welcome every customer and make them feel right at home
- Get to know what they're looking for — and help them find it
- Share your product knowledge with confidence and warmth
- Work towards your own sales goals as part of a supportive team
- Keep our store looking as good as the jewellery in it

## What We're Looking For

- Strong customer-service skills
- Confident and engaging with customers
- Proactive, energetic and motivated
- Excellent communication skills
- Reliable and able to work effectively as part of a team
- Strong attention to detail and professional presentation
- Full availability
- Previous retail or sales experience preferred
- A proven ability to meet sales targets/KPIs highly desirable

## Why Join Us

Be part of a team that's been helping people celebrate life's biggest moments for years. You'll get the training, support and product knowledge to build a genuine career in retail — not just a job.
