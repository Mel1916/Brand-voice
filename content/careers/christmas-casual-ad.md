## About the Role

Christmas is our biggest season — and we want two more friendly faces on the floor to help us make it magical for every customer. As a Christmas Casual at York Jewellers, you'll help customers find the perfect gift (or the perfect treat for themselves), all while soaking up the buzz of our busiest time of year.

This is a casual role for the Christmas trading period — a great opportunity if you love retail, thrive on a bit of festive energy, and want real experience in a premium environment.

## What You'll Do

- Welcome every customer and help them find exactly what they're looking for
- Recommend pieces that suit their needs and the occasion
- Get to know our range so you can talk about it with confidence
- Give great service at the till, every time
- Help keep the store looking its festive best
- Pitch in wherever the team needs you during our busiest weeks

## What We're Looking For

- Friendly, energetic and customer-focused
- Willingness to learn and a good listener
- Confident and approachable
- Reliable and flexible with availability
- Comfortable working in a fast-paced retail environment
- Strong communication and teamwork skills
- Professional presentation
- Previous retail or customer-service experience preferred
- Full availability throughout the Christmas trading period

## Why Join Us

Get a front-row seat to our busiest, most exciting season — with the training and support to build real retail experience (and maybe a future with us beyond Christmas).
