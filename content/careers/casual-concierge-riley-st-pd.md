## Position Purpose

The Concierge is the first point of contact for customers at our new 22 Riley St store, responsible for creating a welcoming, professional first impression and helping customers navigate the store and its services during the store's opening period.

## Key Responsibilities

- Greet and welcome all customers entering the store
- Provide customers with information and direct them to the appropriate product, service or team member
- Support the retail team on the floor, particularly during peak trading periods
- Maintain a high standard of store presentation
- Assist with the smooth day-to-day operation of the store, including during the Christmas trading period
- Represent the store professionally at all times in line with company standards

## Key Selection Criteria

**Essential**
- Friendly, approachable and confident personality
- Energetic and enthusiastic approach to work
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
- Comfortable engaging with customers and creating a welcoming experience
- Reliable and punctual
- Able to stand and remain active for extended periods
- Available Thursday through to Sunday for the duration of the role

**Desirable**
- Previous experience in retail, hospitality, customer service or concierge roles

## Physical Requirements

- Extended periods of standing and remaining active on the shop floor
