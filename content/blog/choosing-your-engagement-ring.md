# Blog — How to Choose an Engagement Ring That Feels Like "Yes"

Choosing an engagement ring can feel like a lot of pressure. Here's the truth: there's no single "right" ring — only the one that's right for the two of you. Let's break down how to get there.

## Start with the story, not the catalogue

Before you look at a single setting, think about what makes your relationship yours. A shared love of travel, an inside joke, a colour that means something — the best rings usually start here, not with a trend list. When you're ready, that story becomes the brief for a bespoke design session with our team.

## Know your certifications

Every diamond and gemstone we sell is certified, so you're never guessing about quality or authenticity. When you're comparing rings — with us or anywhere else — ask to see the certification. It's the clearest way to know exactly what you're buying.

## Decide: ready-made or build your own?

- **Ready-made** — Browse our collection and find a setting that's already speaking to you.
- **Build your own** — Start from scratch: metal, setting, centre stone, all chosen by you, guided by us.

Neither is "more romantic" than the other. What matters is that it feels right for you.

## Get the fit right — and keep it right

Rings get resized. Fingers change. That's why complimentary resizing comes standard with every piece we sell — so the fit is never the thing standing between you and wearing it every day.

## Ask questions. That's what we're here for.

If you're not sure where to start, that's completely normal. Book an appointment at our Westfield Penrith store and talk it through with our team — no pressure, just a conversation about what you're looking for.
